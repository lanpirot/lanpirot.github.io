Research

Automated merge conflict resolution

Three-way textual merge still hands too many conflicts back to developers and agents, so I study where it fails and build techniques that resolve conflicts semantically instead of line by line.

From merging to software variability

A branch is just a software variant that hasn't been integrated yet, and I carry results between version control and product line engineering in both directions.

Model-based software engineering with MATLAB/Simulink

Empirical Simulink research is starved for data, so I collect and index public models, synthesize realistic ones, and anonymize industrial models so companies can share them.
